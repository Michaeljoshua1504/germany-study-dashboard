🧹

🔥
Heat the room during winter even when absent (Oct–Apr). Failure to do so causing damage = you pay for repairs.
💧
Run hot water for 5 min after 72+ hour absence to prevent Legionella (required by German law, DIN 1988).

No pets without landlord's prior written consent.

🪟

🚨
Report any damage in writing immediately. Delay = you become liable for consequential damage even if you didn't cause the original problem.

Notify arrival 7 days in advance — both to Mr. Wittmann AND the Housing Office. Fill in the Hof University arrival form.
